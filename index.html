Common questions

Why does Memory Wall ask for my whole photo library?

“Random memories” spreads a selection across everything you have, rather than showing only the newest pictures — that needs access to the library as a whole. If you would rather not grant it, use Pick photos instead: that uses the system picker and works perfectly with limited access.

Do my photographs leave my device?

No. They are read from your library, drawn into the room, and that is all. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The one thing that does leave is the place written inside a picture: if a photograph records where it was taken, those coordinates go to Apple to be turned into a name for the caption. The picture itself never goes anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I play my own music?

Three ways. Pick something from your library; search Apple Music, if you have a subscription; or simply start something in the Music app before you walk in and choose Keep what’s playing in Music — the room’s controls will drive it.

Searching Apple Music finds nothing

Catalogue search needs an Apple Music subscription. Without one, the search looks through the music in your own library only, and says so. Note that streaming a song without adding it to your Library does not put it in your library.

Why does the Vision Pro version ask about world sensing?

For one thing only: so that a photograph you tap comes and stands squarely in front of wherever you are actually looking. Without it the picture arrives off to one side and foreshortened. No camera data is used, and nothing is recorded.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and Apple Vision Pro running visionOS 2 or later. One purchase covers all of them.

My portrait photos look cropped

In the Mosaic and the Sphere every tile has to be the same shape, or the grid would not close around you. The whole photograph is shown inside the tile, with the space left over filled by a blurred copy of the picture — nothing is cut. Tap a photograph to bring it closer and it takes its own shape again.
