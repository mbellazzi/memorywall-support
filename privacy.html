Privacy Policy

Memory Wall · Last updated 23 October 2026

Memory Wall does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data.

There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server. The developer cannot see anything you do in the app.

Your photographs

Memory Wall asks for access to your photo library so that it can build a room out of your pictures. Those pictures are read from the library on your device, drawn into the room, and never leave the device. Nothing is uploaded, copied to any other service, or retained after you close the app.

If you would rather not grant access to the whole library, you can use the system photo picker instead and choose only the pictures you want to see.

Your music

Memory Wall can play music while you are inside a room. It does this in one of three ways, and in every case the music is played by Apple’s own frameworks on your device:

  • from your own music library, through the system music picker;
  • by searching Apple Music, if you have an Apple Music subscription;
  • by adopting whatever the Music app is already playing.

Memory Wall does not read, export or store your listening history. Apple’s own handling of Apple Music is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Apple Vision Pro

On Apple Vision Pro, Memory Wall uses ARKit to read the position of your head, so that a photograph you choose comes and stands squarely in front of you. This information is used only in the moment, only to place the picture, and is never recorded or transmitted. Memory Wall does not use the cameras and receives no image of your surroundings.

Children

Memory Wall collects no data from anyone, including children.
